## 🗼 TOKYO — 7 Days

> Includes 1 day trip to Nikko + 1 day trip to Yokohama (5 days in the city itself)

### Why 7 days
Tokyo is enormous. Even with a tight itinerary, the sheer size of the city means each neighborhood is a half or full day on its own. With Ghibli Museum, Akihabara, teamLab, DisneySea, and the main sights, 7 days is a comfortable pace.

### Suggested Day Breakdown
| Day | Focus | Key POIs |
|---|---|---|
| Day 1 | East Tokyo (temples + old city) | Senso-ji, Kaminarimon, Nakamise, Ueno Park, Ameyoko Market |
| Day 2 | Anime & Pop Culture | Akihabara, Nakano Broadway, Pokemon Center |
| Day 3 | West Tokyo (parks + shrines) | Meiji Jingu, Yoyogi Park, Harajuku, Shibuya Crossing, Shibuya Sky |
| Day 4 | Ghibli + teamLab | Ghibli Museum (morning), teamLab Planets or Borderless (evening) |
| Day 5 | Day trip → Nikko | Tosho-gu, Rinnoji, Kegon Falls, Lake Chuzenji |
| Day 6 | Day trip → Yokohama | Chinatown, Cup Noodles Museum, Sankeien Garden, Landmark Tower |
| Day 7 | Modern Tokyo + Judo | Kodokan Judo Institute, Tokyo Skytree, Odaiba, Omoide Yokocho |

### Time budget check
| POI | Est. Time |
|---|---|
| Senso-ji + Nakamise + Asakusa stroll | 2 hrs |
| Ueno Park + Ameyoko | 2 hrs |
| Akihabara deep dive | 4–5 hrs |
| Nakano Broadway | 2–3 hrs |
| Meiji Jingu + Harajuku | 2 hrs |
| Shibuya area (crossing + Sky) | 3 hrs |
| Ghibli Museum | 2–3 hrs |
| teamLab Planets | 1.5–2 hrs |
| Kodokan Judo Institute | 1–2 hrs |
| Tokyo Skytree | 1.5–2 hrs |
| Nikko (full day trip) | 8–9 hrs incl. transit |
| Yokohama (full day trip) | 8 hrs incl. transit |

**Total across 7 days: ~40–45 hrs of POI time → comfortable at ~6–7 hrs/day**

## 🏯 KYOTO — 5 Days

> Includes 1 day trip to Nara (4 days in Kyoto itself)

### Why 5 days
Kyoto has the highest density of UNESCO World Heritage sites in Japan. Fushimi Inari alone takes a half day. The city rewards a slow pace — rushing it is one of the most common Japan travel regrets.

### Suggested Day Breakdown
| Day | Focus | Key POIs |
|---|---|---|
| Day 1 | Southern Kyoto (shrines) | Fushimi Inari Taisha (early morning), To-ji Temple, Nishiki Market |
| Day 2 | Higashiyama (eastern temples) | Kiyomizu-dera, Sanjusangen-do, Ginkaku-ji, Philosopher's Path |
| Day 3 | Northern Kyoto | Kinkaku-ji, Ryoan-ji, Daitoku-ji, Nijo-jo Castle |
| Day 4 | Arashiyama + Gion | Bamboo Grove (dawn), Sagano Train, Tenryu-ji, Gion at dusk |
| Day 5 | Day trip → Nara | Nara Park, Todai-ji, Kasuga Taisha, Kofuku-ji |

### Time budget check
| POI | Est. Time |
|---|---|
| Fushimi Inari (full mountain) | 3–4 hrs |
| Kinkaku-ji | 1–1.5 hrs |
| Kiyomizu-dera + surrounding streets | 2 hrs |
| Philosopher's Path walk | 1–1.5 hrs |
| Arashiyama Bamboo Grove | 1 hr |
| Nijo-jo Castle | 1.5–2 hrs |
| Gion evening stroll | 1.5–2 hrs |
| Nara full day trip | 6–7 hrs incl. transit |

**Total across 5 days: ~30–35 hrs of POI time → ~6–7 hrs/day**

## 🦌 NARA — Day Trip (from Kyoto)

### Why 1 day
Nara's must-see spots are clustered tightly around the park. A dedicated day lets you visit all key temples and spend real time with the deer without rushing.

### Suggested Schedule
| Time | Activity |
|---|---|
| 8:00 | Depart Kyoto (30 min by express train) |
| 8:45 | Arrive Nara; walk to Nara Park — deer are most active in the morning |
| 9:00 | Nara Park + deer crackers |
| 10:30 | Todai-ji Temple (Great Buddha) |
| 12:00 | Lunch near Nishiki-koji area |
| 13:00 | Kasuga Taisha Shrine |
| 14:00 | Kofuku-ji Temple + Sarusawa Pond |
| 15:00 | Isuien Garden (optional) |
| 16:00 | Return to Kyoto |

## 🏙️ OSAKA — 3 Days

### Why 3 days
Osaka's main draw is street food and entertainment. USJ alone requires a full day. The castle + Dotonbori fills another. The third day covers the Namba food scene and Umeda.

### Suggested Day Breakdown
| Day | Focus | Key POIs |
|---|---|---|
| Day 1 | USJ — full day | Universal Studios Japan, Nintendo World |
| Day 2 | History + street food | Osaka Castle, Shinsekai, Dotonbori, Kuromon Market |
| Day 3 | Food + architecture | Tenjinbashisuji, Umeda Sky Building, Osaka Aquarium |

### Time budget check
| POI | Est. Time |
|---|---|
| USJ (full day) | 8–10 hrs |
| Osaka Castle | 1.5–2 hrs |
| Dotonbori + street food | 2–3 hrs |
| Kuromon Market | 1 hr |
| Osaka Aquarium | 2–3 hrs |
| Umeda Sky Building | 1 hr |

**Total: ~18–22 hrs across 3 days → ~6–7 hrs/day**

## 🌊 HIROSHIMA & MIYAJIMA — 2 Days

### Why 2 days
One day for Hiroshima city (Peace Park + Museum is emotionally heavy — don't rush it), one full day for Miyajima island. Trying to do both in one day is too rushed.

### Suggested Day Breakdown
| Day | Focus | Key POIs |
|---|---|---|
| Day 1 | Hiroshima city | Peace Memorial Park, Atomic Bomb Dome, Museum, Hiroshima Castle, Shukkeien Garden |
| Day 2 | Miyajima Island | Ferry to island, Itsukushima Shrine, Daisho-in Temple, oyster lunch, hike Mt. Misen (optional) |

### Time budget check
| POI | Est. Time |
|---|---|
| Peace Memorial Park + Dome | 1.5–2.5 hrs |
| Peace Memorial Museum | 1.5–2 hrs |
| Hiroshima Castle | 1 hr |
| Miyajima full island day | 6–8 hrs |
| Ferry to/from Miyajima | 1 hr round trip |

## 🦌 NIKKO — Day Trip (from Tokyo)

### Why 1 day
Nikko's shrines are clustered in one area at the base of the mountains. A full day is enough to visit Tosho-gu, the other shrines, and Kegon Falls with a bus ride up to Lake Chuzenji.

### Suggested Schedule
| Time | Activity |
|---|---|
| 7:00 | Depart Tokyo (Asakusa Station → Tobu Nikko, 2 hrs) |
| 9:30 | Arrive Nikko; walk up to shrine complex |
| 10:00 | Rinnoji Temple |
| 10:45 | Tosho-gu Shrine (main complex) |
| 12:30 | Lunch near Shinkyo Bridge area |
| 13:30 | Bus up to Chuzenji-ko (Lake Chuzenji) |
| 14:00 | Kegon Waterfall (elevator) + lake walk |
| 15:30 | Bus back to Nikko town |
| 16:00 | Taiyuin Mausoleum (quieter; don't skip) |
| 17:00 | Train back to Tokyo |

## 🦑 YOKOHAMA — Day Trip (from Tokyo)

### Why 1 day
Only 30 minutes from central Tokyo; a relaxed day covers Chinatown, Cup Noodles Museum, Sankeien Garden, and the waterfront with time to spare.

### Suggested Schedule
| Time | Activity |
|---|---|
| 9:00 | Depart Tokyo (Shinjuku or Shibuya → Yokohama, 30 min) |
| 9:45 | Sankeien Garden (peaceful morning visit) |
| 11:30 | Yokohama Chinatown (lunch + explore) |
| 13:30 | Cup Noodles Museum (invent your own flavor) |
| 15:00 | Osanbashi Pier + waterfront walk |
| 16:00 | Yokohama Landmark Tower observatory |
| 17:30 | Return to Tokyo |

## 🏝️ OKINAWA — 4 Days

### Why 4 days
Okinawa is completely different from mainland Japan — distinct culture, food, and landscape. The main island needs 2 days; a day trip to Kerama Islands is essential for snorkeling; the northern Yanbaru area needs a separate day.

### Suggested Day Breakdown
| Day | Focus | Key POIs |
|---|---|---|
| Day 1 | Naha city | Shuri Castle, Kokusai-dori, Makishi Public Market, Seifa-utaki |
| Day 2 | Central Okinawa | Nakagusuku Castle Ruins, Cape Manzamo, American Village area |
| Day 3 | Kerama Islands day trip | Ferry from Naha; snorkeling, beaches, coral reefs |
| Day 4 | North — Yanbaru | Yanbaru National Park, Cape Hedo (northernmost point), scenic drive |

### Time budget check
| POI | Est. Time |
|---|---|
| Shuri Castle | 1.5–2 hrs |
| Kokusai-dori + Makishi Market | 2 hrs |
| Seifa-utaki | 1 hr |
| Nakagusuku Ruins | 1 hr |
| Cape Manzamo | 45 min |
| Kerama Islands (day trip) | 6–8 hrs |
| Yanbaru NP | 4–5 hrs |

## 🔥 NAGASAKI — 2 Days

### Why 2 days
Nagasaki's atomic bomb sites need at least a half day of respectful, unhurried time. The historical European quarter (Glover, Dejima, Dutch Slopes) fills a solid second day.

### Suggested Day Breakdown
| Day | Focus | Key POIs |
|---|---|---|
| Day 1 | Atomic Bomb history | Atomic Bomb Museum, Peace Park, Urakami Cathedral, bombing hypocenter |
| Day 2 | Historical Nagasaki | Glover Garden, Dutch Slopes, Dejima Island, Sofuku-ji Temple |

---

## 🍣 KANAZAWA — 2 Days

### Why 2 days
Often called "Little Kyoto," Kanazawa is compact but rich. Two days lets you cover Kenroku-en, the castle, Higashi Chaya, the 21st Century Museum, and Omicho Market at a relaxed pace.

### Suggested Day Breakdown
| Day | Focus | Key POIs |
|---|---|---|
| Day 1 | Garden + castle + market | Kenroku-en Garden (morning), Kanazawa Castle Park, Omicho Market (lunch), Higashi Chaya (afternoon/evening) |
| Day 2 | Art + culture | 21st Century Museum of Contemporary Art, Nagamachi Samurai District, Nishi Chaya District |

---

## 🗼 TOKYO FINAL — 3 Days (Buffer)

### Why 3 days
- **Day 1:** Any POIs you missed in the first Tokyo week; Shinjuku shopping
- **Day 2:** DisneySea (if not done), Kabuki-za, or Roppongi
- **Day 3:** Packing, flight prep, Narita/Haneda transfer

> Never schedule your last day before a flight as a full POI day — leave it as a buffer.
