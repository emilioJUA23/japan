## 🗼 TOKYO — 7 Days

> Includes 1 day trip to Nikko and 1 martial-arts day with the January Grand Sumo tournament

### Why 7 days
Tokyo is enormous. Each neighborhood is a half or full day on its own. Winter makes a 7-day first stay work well: short daylight pushes you indoors (museums, Akihabara, teamLab), and the arrival lines up with the **January sumo tournament** (expected Jan 9–23, 2028).

### Suggested Day Breakdown
| Day   | Date    | Focus                           | Key POIs                                                              |
| ----- | ------- | ------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Day 1 | Jan 8   | Arrival                         | Airport, Suica, neighborhood walk                                     |
| Day 2 | Jan 9   | East Tokyo (temples + old city) | Senso-ji, Nakamise, Ueno Park, Ameyoko, Skytree at sunset             |
| Day 3 | Jan 10  | West Tokyo + Coming of Age Day  | Meiji Jingu (archery ritual), Harajuku, Shibuya Crossing, Shibuya Sky |
| Day 4 | Jan 11  | Anime & Pop Culture             | Akihabara, Nakano Broadway                                            |
| Day 5 | Jan 12  | Ghibli + teamLab                | Ghibli Museum (morning), teamLab Planets or Borderless (evening)      |
| Day 6 | Jan 13  | Day trip → Nikko                | Tosho-gu, Taiyuin, Rinnoji, frozen Kegon Falls, Lake Chuzenji         |
| Day 7 | Jan 14  | 🥋 Martial Arts                 | Kodokan Judo Institute, chanko nabe in Ryogoku, **Grand Sumo**        |

### Time budget check
| POI | Est. Time |
|---|---|
| Senso-ji + Nakamise + Asakusa stroll | 2 hrs |
| Ueno Park + Ameyoko | 2 hrs |
| Tokyo Skytree | 1.5–2 hrs |
| Akihabara deep dive | 4–5 hrs |
| Nakano Broadway | 2–3 hrs |
| Meiji Jingu + Harajuku | 2 hrs |
| Shibuya area (crossing + Sky) | 3 hrs |

| Kodokan Judo Institute | 1–2 hrs |
| Sumo tournament (lower divisions → top division) | 4 hrs |
| Nikko (full day trip) | 8–9 hrs incl. transit |

**Total across 7 days: ~40–46 hrs of POI time → ~6–7 hrs/day**

## ⛩️ NAGANO & SHIBU ONSEN — 1 Day (Overnight)

### Why 1 day
Nagano sits on the Hokuriku Shinkansen between Tokyo and Hakuba. It's the natural stop for Zenko-ji and the Snow Monkeys, and a ryokan night in Shibu Onsen is one of the best winter experiences in Japan.

### Suggested Schedule
| Time | Activity |
|---|---|
| 8:30 | Shinkansen Tokyo → Nagano (~1h20m) |
| 10:15 | Zenko-ji Temple + Okaidan tunnel |
| 12:00 | Shinshu soba lunch |
| 13:00 | Nagaden train to Yudanaka (~45 min) |
| 14:45 | **Jigokudani Snow Monkey Park** (⚠️ advance ticket required in winter) |
| 17:00 | Shibu Onsen ryokan: 9 public baths, kaiseki dinner |

## 🏂 HAKUBA — 4 Days

### Why 4 days
Hakuba Valley has 10 ski resorts on one lift ticket, linked by free shuttle buses. Four nights gives a half-day to gear up and warm up, then **3 full days** on three different mountains. That's enough to progress without wrecking your legs before the long sightseeing stretch that follows.

### Suggested Day Breakdown
| Day | Date | Focus | Resort |
|---|---|---|---|
| Day 9 | Jan 16 | Travel from Shibu Onsen + gear rental + afternoon session (or lesson) | Happo-one (lower slopes) |
| Day 10 | Jan 17 | Full day: Hakuba's biggest mountain, Olympic runs | Happo-one |
| Day 11 | Jan 18 | Full day: terrain park + long cruisers; optional night riding | Hakuba 47 & Goryu |
| Day 12 | Jan 19 | Full day: gentle and wide, or deep powder trees | Tsugaike Kogen or Cortina |

### Skill-level guidance
| Level | Suggestion |
|---|---|
| Never snowboarded | Half-day lesson D9 + full-day lesson D10, then practice D11–D12 on Tsugaike/Goryu's gentle runs |
| A few days before | Short refresher on D9 afternoon; mix groomers and the terrain park |
| Confident rider | Chase powder: Cortina and Happo-one's upper mountain on fresh-snow mornings |

## 🍣 KANAZAWA — 3 Days

> Includes 1 day trip to Shirakawa-go

### Why 3 days
Two days for Kanazawa itself (Kenroku-en with winter **yukitsuri** ropes, geisha and samurai districts, the 21st Century Museum), plus a full day for the snowbound UNESCO village of **Shirakawa-go**. Winter is also **snow crab season**, the best time of year to eat here.

### Suggested Day Breakdown
| Day | Date | Focus | Key POIs |
|---|---|---|---|
| Day 13 | Jan 20 | Travel from Hakuba + garden + castle | Omicho Market lunch, Kenroku-en, Kanazawa Castle Park, crab dinner |
| Day 14 | Jan 21 | Art + culture | 21st Century Museum, Higashi Chaya, Nagamachi Samurai District, Nishi Chaya |
| Day 15 | Jan 22 | Day trip → Shirakawa-go | Shiroyama Viewpoint, Wada House, Myozen-ji |

> 🌙 **Shirakawa-go light-up** happens on only ~4 evenings per season and is reservation-only. The 2028 dates are announced around July 2027. If one falls on Jan 20–22, swap D14 and D15.

## 🏯 KYOTO — 6 Days

> Includes 1 day trip to Nara

### Why 6 days
Kyoto has the highest density of UNESCO World Heritage sites in Japan. In January it's at its **least crowded** (except for the Chinese New Year bump around Jan 26), and a light snowfall on Kinkaku-ji is a rare bonus if you get lucky.

### Suggested Day Breakdown
| Day | Date | Focus | Key POIs |
|---|---|---|---|
| Day 16 | Jan 23 | Travel from Kanazawa + Fushimi Inari | Fushimi Inari Taisha (sunset from Yotsutsuji) |
| Day 17 | Jan 24 | Higashiyama (eastern temples) | Kiyomizu-dera, Sanjusangen-do, Ginkaku-ji, Philosopher's Path, Gion |
| Day 18 | Jan 25 | Northern Kyoto | Kinkaku-ji, Ryoan-ji, Daitoku-ji, Nijo-jo Castle |
| Day 19 | Jan 26 | Arashiyama + Gion | Bamboo Grove (dawn), Tenryu-ji, Okochi Sanso, Gion at dusk |
| Day 20 | Jan 27 | Day trip → Nara | Nara Park, Todai-ji, Kasuga Taisha, Kofuku-ji |
| Day 21 | Jan 28 | Hidden Kyoto + mountain villages | Kurama & Kibune (icy trail: check conditions), Pontocho dinner |

> ❄️ The **Sagano Romantic Train doesn't run in January–February**, so Okochi Sanso Villa replaces it on D19.

### Time budget check
| POI | Est. Time |
|---|---|
| Fushimi Inari (to Yotsutsuji + back) | 2–3 hrs |
| Kinkaku-ji | 1–1.5 hrs |
| Kiyomizu-dera + surrounding streets | 2 hrs |
| Philosopher's Path walk | 1–1.5 hrs |
| Arashiyama Bamboo Grove | 1 hr |
| Nijo-jo Castle | 1.5–2 hrs |
| Gion evening stroll | 1.5–2 hrs |
| Kibune & Kurama half-day | 3–4 hrs |
| Nara full day trip | 6–7 hrs incl. transit |

**Total across 6 days: ~34–40 hrs of POI time → ~6 hrs/day (fits the shorter winter daylight)**

## 🦌 NARA — Day Trip (from Kyoto)

### Why 1 day
Nara's must-see spots are clustered tightly around the park. A dedicated day lets you visit all key temples and spend real time with the deer without rushing.

### Suggested Schedule
| Time | Activity |
|---|---|
| 8:00 | Depart Kyoto (JR Nara Line, ~45 min) |
| 9:00 | Nara Park + deer crackers |
| 10:30 | Todai-ji Temple (Great Buddha) |
| 12:00 | Lunch near Higashimuki arcade |
| 13:00 | Kasuga Taisha Shrine |
| 14:00 | Kofuku-ji Temple + Sarusawa Pond |
| 15:00 | Isuien Garden (optional) |
| 16:15 | Return to Kyoto |

## 🏙️ OSAKA — 3 Days

### Why 3 days
Osaka's main draw is street food and entertainment. USJ alone needs a full day; it's placed on a **Monday** to dodge weekend crowds.

### Suggested Day Breakdown
| Day | Date | Focus | Key POIs |
|---|---|---|---|
| Day 22 | Jan 29 | Travel + street food | Kuromon Market, Dotonbori, Shinsaibashi, food crawl |
| Day 23 | Jan 30 | History + aquarium + views | Osaka Castle (plum grove), Shinsekai, Kaiyukan Aquarium, Umeda Sky |
| Day 24 | Jan 31 | USJ — full day | Universal Studios Japan, Super Nintendo World |

---

## 🌊 HIROSHIMA & MIYAJIMA — 2 Days

### Why 2 days
One day for Hiroshima city (the Peace Park and Museum are emotionally heavy, so don't rush them), one full day for Miyajima island. **January–February is peak oyster season** here.

### Suggested Day Breakdown
| Day | Date | Focus | Key POIs |
|---|---|---|---|
| Day 25 | Feb 1 | Hiroshima city | Peace Memorial Park, Atomic Bomb Dome, Museum, Hiroshima Castle |
| Day 26 | Feb 2 | Miyajima Island | Itsukushima Shrine, Daisho-in, oyster lunch, Mt. Misen ropeway |

---

## 🗼 TOKYO FINAL — 2 Days (Buffer)

### Why 2 days
- **Day 27 (Feb 3):** Nozomi back from Hiroshima (~4h), then **Setsubun** bean-throwing at Senso-ji or Zojo-ji
- **Day 28 (Feb 4):** Final shopping or Yanaka morning, pack, evening flight home

> Never schedule your last day before a flight as a full POI day. Leave it as a buffer.
