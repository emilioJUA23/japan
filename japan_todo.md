## 💉 VACCINES & HEALTH

> Japan requires **no vaccinations** for entry. The items below are travel medicine recommendations.
> See a travel medicine clinic ideally **6–8 weeks before departure** → by **November 2027**.

- [ ] **Book travel medicine appointment** — by November 2027 at the latest
- [ ] **Hepatitis A** — recommended for all travelers; 2-dose series (0 + 6–12 months); if unvaccinated, start by **mid-2027** for full protection
- [ ] **Hepatitis B** — recommended; 3-dose series over 6 months → start by **mid-2027** if unvaccinated
- [ ] **Tetanus / Tdap** — confirm routine vaccination is up to date (booster every 10 years)
- [ ] **Japanese Encephalitis** — mosquito-borne, so risk is minimal in a Japanese winter; ask the clinic, likely unnecessary
- [ ] **Typhoid** — low risk in Japan but sometimes recommended for peace of mind; oral or injection
- [ ] **COVID-19** — Japan has no entry requirements; keep routine vaccination current per your own guidelines
- [ ] **Pack any prescription medications** with 30+ days supply; bring a copy of each prescription in case of checks
- [ ] **Basic travel pharmacy** — antihistamine, antidiarrheal, pain reliever, blister treatment, sunscreen (snow glare), lip balm + moisturizer (dry winter air), ibuprofen for snowboarding soreness
