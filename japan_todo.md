## 🛂 PASSPORT, VISAS & ENTRY

### Japanese Entry
- [ ] **Check passport expiry** — must be valid for the full duration of stay; ideally valid through October 2027 or beyond
- [x] ~~Apply for Japan visa~~ — **Not required.** Guatemalan passport is visa-free for up to 90 days ✅
- [ ] **Register on Visit Japan Web** (free) — Japan's pre-arrival immigration portal; speeds up customs on arrival → https://vjw-lp.digital.go.jp/en/
- [ ] Note your **first hotel address** on hand — required on the Japan arrival card

### US Transit ⚠️ Critical
> Guatemala is **NOT** on the US Visa Waiver Program. Even an airside connection through LAX, DFW, or IAH requires a valid US visa.
- [ ] **Confirm you have a valid US B-1/B-2 visa** (or C-1 transit visa) before booking a US-hub itinerary
  - If your US visa is expired or you don't have one, consider routing through **Mexico City (MEX)** with ANA or JAL — no Mexican visa needed for Guatemalans
  - Alternative: route through **Seoul (ICN)** on Korean Air or **Taipei (TPE)** on EVA Air — no visa needed for Guatemalans in either country
- [ ] Check US visa **expiration date** is beyond your travel dates if you already have one

## 💉 VACCINES & HEALTH

> Japan requires **no vaccinations** for entry. The items below are travel medicine recommendations.
> See a travel medicine clinic ideally **6–8 weeks before departure** → by **January 2027**.

- [ ] **Book travel medicine appointment** — by January 2027 at the latest
- [ ] **Hepatitis A** — recommended for all travelers; 2-dose series (0 + 6–12 months); if unvaccinated, start by **Sep 2026** for full protection
- [ ] **Hepatitis B** — recommended; 3-dose series over 6 months → start by **Sep 2026** if unvaccinated
- [ ] **Tetanus / Tdap** — confirm routine vaccination is up to date (booster every 10 years)
- [ ] **Japanese Encephalitis** — consider if spending time outdoors in rural areas (Okinawa/Yanbaru NP, Nikko); 2-dose series 28 days apart → start by **Feb 2027**
- [ ] **Typhoid** — low risk in Japan but sometimes recommended for peace of mind; oral or injection
- [ ] **COVID-19** — Japan has no entry requirements; keep routine vaccination current per your own guidelines
- [ ] **Pack any prescription medications** with 30+ days supply; bring a copy of each prescription in case of checks
- [ ] **Basic travel pharmacy** — antihistamine, antidiarrheal, pain reliever, blister treatment, sunscreen
