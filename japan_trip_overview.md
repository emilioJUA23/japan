# 🗾 Japan — Month-Long Trip Overview

> **Duration:** ~30 days | **Traveler:** Luis | **Status:** Planning

---

## 📅 Best Time to Visit

| Season | Months | Highlights | Watch Out |
|--------|--------|------------|-----------|
| 🌸 Spring | Late Mar – Apr | Cherry blossoms (sakura); festivals | Crowds + high prices |
| 🌿 Early Summer | May – June | Green mountains; fewer tourists | Rainy season starts June |
| ☀️ Summer | July – Aug | Festivals, fireworks, beaches (Okinawa) | Very hot & humid |
| 🍁 Autumn | Oct – Nov | Foliage (koyo); comfortable temps | Popular = crowds |
| ❄️ Winter | Dec – Feb | Snow in north; fewer tourists; ski season | Cold in Honshu |

**Sweet spots:** Late March–April (sakura) or October–November (foliage) — both are peak but worth it.

```
Week 1 → TOKYO (base)
         └─ Day trips: Nikko, Kamakura, Yokohama

Week 2 → HAKONE → KYOTO (base)

         OR HOKKAIDO (if nature / food)

```

## 🚄 Transportation

### JR Pass
- **Buy before leaving** — must be purchased outside Japan
- 21-day or 30-day options; covers Shinkansen (most lines), JR trains, some buses/ferries
- ~¥100,000–¥120,000 for 30 days — worth it for long routes
- Does **NOT** cover: Nozomi/Mizuho Shinkansen, Tokyo Metro, private lines

### IC Card (Suica / Pasmo)
- Load cash onto it; use for metro, buses, convenience stores, vending machines
- Get at airport on arrival — **essential**

### Domestic Flights
- Consider flying to Okinawa or Hokkaido (faster & sometimes cheaper than JR)
- Airlines: ANA, JAL, Peach, Jetstar Japan

## 🏠 Accommodation Types

| Type | Cost/Night | Best For |
|------|-----------|---------|
| Business Hotel (Toyoko Inn, APA) | ¥6,000–¥10,000 | Budget; clean; central |
| Capsule Hotel | ¥3,000–¥6,000 | Solo; experience; storage |
| Airbnb / Weekly Apartment | ¥5,000–¥12,000 | Month-long stays; kitchen |
| Ryokan (Traditional Inn) | ¥15,000–¥50,000 | Cultural experience; includes meals |
| Guesthouse / Hostel | ¥2,500–¥5,000 | Budget; meet travelers |

**Tip for a month-long trip:** Mix it up — capsule for a night or two, ryokan 1–2 times in Hakone/Kyoto, apartment rental for a week-long base.

## 📱 Essentials

- [ ] **Pocket WiFi or SIM card** — rent at airport (IIJmio, Sakura Mobile)
- [ ] **JR Pass** — buy before leaving your country
- [ ] **Suica/Pasmo card** — load at airport
- [ ] **Google Maps** — works great in Japan; offline maps too
- [ ] **Hyperdia or Navitime** — train routing
- [ ] **Google Translate** — camera translation for menus & signs
- [ ] **PayPay / cash** — Japan is still very cash-heavy; always carry ¥10,000+
- [ ] **7-Eleven / FamilyMart / Lawson ATMs** — accept foreign cards
