## 📅 Best Time to Visit

| Season | Months | Highlights | Watch Out |
|--------|--------|------------|-----------|
| 🌸 Spring | Late Mar – Apr | Cherry blossoms (sakura); festivals | Crowds + high prices |
| 🌿 Early Summer | May – June | Green mountains; fewer tourists | Rainy season starts June |
| ☀️ Summer | July – Aug | Festivals, fireworks, beaches | Very hot & humid |
| 🍁 Autumn | Oct – Nov | Foliage (koyo); comfortable temps | Popular = crowds |
| ❄️ Winter ← **this trip** | Dec – Feb | Snow in north; fewer tourists; ski season; January sumo | Cold in Honshu; short days; Chinese New Year crowds |

**Why winter:** world-class powder in Hakuba, the January Grand Sumo tournament, snow on Kyoto temples and Shirakawa-go, the emptiest Kyoto of the year, and peak crab and oyster season.
**Chosen window:** mid-January (after the New Year holiday peak) through early February.

## 🗺️ Suggested Month-Long Route

```
Week 1   → TOKYO (7 days)
           └─ Day trip: Nikko · Sumo tournament day
Week 2   → NAGANO (1 night — Zenko-ji, Snow Monkeys, Shibu Onsen ryokan)
           → HAKUBA (4 nights — 🏂 snowboarding)
           → KANAZAWA (3 nights)
              └─ Day trip: Shirakawa-go
Week 3–4 → KYOTO (6 nights)
              └─ Day trip: Nara
           → OSAKA (3 nights) → HIROSHIMA + MIYAJIMA (2 nights)
           → TOKYO (1 night — Setsubun, then fly home)
```

> One continuous line by rail: Hokuriku Shinkansen Tokyo → Nagano → Kanazawa, then Kanazawa → Tsuruga → Kyoto, then the Tokaido/Sanyo Shinkansen. **No domestic flights.**

## 🚄 Transportation

### JR Pass — ❌ Not worth it for this route
- **There is no 30-day JR Pass.** The options are 7/14/21 days (~¥50,000 / ¥80,000 / ¥100,000)
- This one-way route costs ~¥65,000–75,000 in point-to-point tickets, **less than the 21-day pass**
- The **Hokuriku Arch Pass** (¥35,000, 7 days, Tokyo ↔ Kanazawa ↔ Osaka via Nagano) doesn't pay off with this pacing either
- Without a JR Pass, **Nozomi** Shinkansen are fine to ride (fastest Tokyo–Osaka–Hiroshima)

### IC Card (Suica / Pasmo)
- Load cash onto it; use for **all** local JR trains, metro, buses, convenience stores, vending machines
- Get at the airport on arrival, or add to Apple Wallet — **essential**

### Buses in the Alps
- **Alpico express bus** Nagano ↔ Hakuba (~70 min); reserve in peak season
- **Hakuba Valley shuttle buses** between the 10 resorts are free with the valley lift ticket
- **Highway bus** Kanazawa ↔ Shirakawa-go (~1h20m); book ahead in winter

### Luggage Forwarding (Takkyubin)
- Send big bags hotel-to-hotel (~¥2,500–3,500, 1–2 days) so you can do Nagano, the snow monkeys and the buses with a daypack

## 📱 Essentials

- [ ] **Pocket WiFi or SIM card** — rent at airport (IIJmio, Sakura Mobile)
- [ ] **Suica/Pasmo card** — load at airport
- [ ] **Google Maps** — works great in Japan; offline maps too
- [ ] **Navitime or Google Maps** — train routing
- [ ] **Winter gear** — thermals, insulated jacket, waterproof boots with grip, gloves, beanie
- [ ] **Travel insurance that covers snowboarding** — many policies exclude winter sports
- [ ] **Google Translate** — camera translation for menus & signs
