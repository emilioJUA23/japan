## 🗓️ Proposed Route & Calendar (43 nights)

> Snow first, then south, then **end in Kyoto + Tokyo** right when the blossoms open.
> The **last ~2 weeks are the flexible part**: shift Kyoto/Tokyo by a few days once the bloom forecast comes out (late Jan 2028).

| # | Dates (2028) | Nights | Base | Highlights |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 0 | Sun Feb 20 | — | ✈️ GUA → Tokyo | Cross the date line — lose a day |
| 1 | Mon Feb 21 | 1 | Tokyo | Land, Suica, early night (jet lag) |
| 2 | Tue Feb 22 – Tue Feb 29 | 8 | 🏂 **Hakuba** | Shinkansen to Nagano + bus; ride ½ day on the 22nd, then 6 full days + 1 rest day (onsen / Otari) |
| 3 | Wed Mar 1 | 1 | Nagano → Shibu Onsen | Zenko-ji, **Snow Monkeys**, ryokan night |
| 4 | Thu Mar 2 – Sat Mar 4 | 3 | Kanazawa | Kenroku-en (plum), Higashi Chaya, Shirakawa-go day trip (still snowy), Hina Matsuri (Mar 3) |
| 5 | Sun Mar 5 – Mon Mar 13 | 9 | Tokyo (main block) | Akihabara, Nakano, Ghibli, teamLab, Kodokan, Nikko day trip, Senso-ji, Shibuya; optional Mito Kairakuen plum festival or Kamakura |
| 6 | Tue Mar 14 – Wed Mar 15 | 2 | Hiroshima | Peace Park + museum, Miyajima |
| 7 | Thu Mar 16 – Mon Mar 20 | 5 | Osaka | 🥋 **March Grand Sumo (Haru Basho)**, USJ / Nintendo World, Dotonbori, Osaka Castle, Himeji day trip |
| 8 | Tue Mar 21 – Wed Mar 29 | 9 | 🌸 **Kyoto** | First blossoms → full bloom: Philosopher's Path, Maruyama Park, Heian Shrine, Arashiyama, Daigo-ji; Nara day trip |
| 9 | Thu Mar 30 – Mon Apr 3 | 5 | 🌸 **Tokyo (final)** | Full bloom: Ueno, Chidorigafuchi (boats), Meguro River, Shinjuku Gyoen, night sakura |
| 10 | Tue Apr 4 | — | ✈️ Tokyo → GUA | Arrive same day (date line) |

**Why this order:**
- Hakuba sits right after arrival, so the heavy snow gear only travels once (forward it by takkyubin and rent gear in Hakuba)
- Kanazawa → Tokyo is only ~2.5 h on the Hokuriku Shinkansen
- Osaka falls inside the **March sumo tournament** (2nd Sunday of March → Mar 12–26, 2028)
- Kyoto usually blooms a few days after Tokyo, and Tokyo stays in bloom ~1 week, so **Kyoto → Tokyo** at the end catches both

## 🌸 Cherry Blossom Timing — the Key Risk

| Year | Tokyo first bloom | Tokyo full bloom | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| Normal (1991–2020 avg) | ~Mar 24 | ~Apr 1 | JMA normals |
| 2025 | Mar 24 | Mar 30 | JMA |
| 2026 | Mar 19 | Mar 28 | JMA |
| Kyoto (typical) | ~2–3 days after Tokyo | ~Mar 31 – Apr 4 | JMA / tenki.jp |

**What this means:**
- End the trip on **~Apr 4** to be safe. Ending on Mar 27 (a 5-week trip) risks only seeing the first blossoms
- The official forecast comes out **late Jan 2028**, *after* flights are booked, so:
  - ✅ Book **free-cancellation** hotels for Kyoto + final Tokyo
  - ✅ Keep the last two blocks swappable (Kyoto ↔ Tokyo order, ±3 days)
  - ✅ If the bloom comes early, move Kyoto earlier and add Osaka/Himeji days at the end
  - ⚠️ If it's a very late year (Apr 5+), Tokyo still shows first blooms, and Kyoto may be only partly in bloom

## ✅ Decision Checklist — Check These Before Deciding

### 1. 🧑‍💼 Personal & work
- [ ] **Can you get ~6 weeks off?** (Feb 20 – Apr 4 → ~31 working days). If not, see "Shorter versions" below
- [ ] Any fixed dates in Guatemala Feb–Apr 2028? (Semana Santa 2028 is Apr 9–16, after you return ✅)
- [ ] Are you OK with a trip that spans two climates (−10 °C in Hakuba → ~15 °C in Kyoto)?

### 2. 💵 Money
- [ ] Is ~$510/month sustainable for 16 months? (Yes, based on your recent months)
- [ ] Do you prefer the extra spend (~$4,400 more than the March 2027 plan) over a cheaper trip sooner?
- [ ] Budget for **inflation to 2028** (~5–10%), already roughly covered by the 10% buffer

### 3. 🛂 Documents & entry
- [ ] **Passport valid until at least Oct 2028** (airlines often want 6 months past return)
- [ ] Japan visa-free stay: up to **90 days** for Guatemalan passports → 43 days is fine ✅ (re-confirm with the Embassy of Japan in Guatemala before booking)
- [ ] **JESTA** (Japan's new online pre-travel authorization): scheduled to launch **by FY2028 (Apr 2028 – Mar 2029)**. Probably not required for a Feb 2028 entry, but **check in late 2027**
- [ ] **US visa** for the US transit (LAX/DFW/IAH) must be valid through **Apr 4, 2028**

### 4. 🌸 Sakura risk
- [ ] Accept that the exact bloom date is only known ~6 weeks before the end of the trip
- [ ] Only free-cancellation bookings for Mar 21 → Apr 3

### 5. 🏂 Snowboarding
- [ ] Your level: lesson needed? (adds up to $150)
- [ ] Travel insurance **explicitly covers snowboarding** (on-piste at minimum)
- [ ] Rent vs. bring gear (renting is recommended; 6 weeks with a board is a pain)

### 6. 🏨 Prices & crowds
- [ ] Late March – early April is **Japan's most expensive and crowded period** (sakura + Japanese spring break). Kyoto hotels can cost 2× normal. Book early with free cancellation
- [ ] Kyoto lodging tax (¥200–¥1,000+/person/night since Mar 2026) applies

### 7. 🧾 Rules that changed recently
- [ ] **Departure tax is now ¥3,000** (since Jul 2026), included in the flight price
- [ ] **Tax-free shopping** became a **refund system on Nov 1, 2026**: you pay 10% tax in the shop and get it back at the airport. Leave extra time at departure and keep purchases accessible

### 8. 🧳 Logistics
- [ ] Luggage for 6 weeks + winter clothes: use **takkyubin** (forward bags Tokyo → Hakuba, and winter gear back to Tokyo/airport)
- [ ] Laundry every ~5–7 days (coin laundries everywhere)
- [ ] Enough prescription medicine for 6+ weeks
